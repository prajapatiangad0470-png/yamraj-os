🏛️ Main Court 📜 Chitragupta 👹 28 Narak 🪪 Death Warrant ⚔️ Yamdoot Forces

1. Tamisra (तामिस्र)

Bound in heavy chains in pitch dark abysses for theft.

2. Andhatamisra (अंधतामिस्र)

Torment for deceivers and betrayers of sacred trust.

3. Raurava (रौरव)

Serpent pit for those who lived purely out of greed.

4. Maharaurava (महारौरव)

Flame caverns for destroyers of innocent life.

5. Kumbhipaka (कुम्भीपाक)

Boiled in giant cauldrons of searing hot lava oil.

6. Kalasutra (कालसूत्र)

Sliced on red hot copper threads for disrespecting elders.

7. Asipatravana (असिपत्रवन)

Hacked by razor blade trees in a burning forest.

8. Vaitarani (वैतरणी)

Swept into boiling rivers of blood, bone and acid.
